Beat admin

Add, reorder and remove beats, and set their prices, without touching GitHub. Changes go live about a minute after you publish.

Connect to GitHub

The token is kept only in this browser and is sent only to GitHub. Use a token that has access to this one repository only.

How do I get a token?
  1. On GitHub, open Settings, then Developer settings, then Personal access tokens, then Fine-grained tokens, then Generate new token.
  2. Give it any name, choose an expiry (for example 90 days), and under Repository access choose "Only select repositories" and pick your site's repository.
  3. Under Permissions, Repository permissions, set Contents to Read and write.
  4. Click Generate token, copy it, and paste it above.

Prices and offers

Set the prices for all beats and the Exclusive offer range
Offers accepted from US$ to US$
This range is shown on every beat in place of the Exclusive price. It is always in US dollars.

These prices apply to every beat. To give one beat its own price, or to set its genre and BPM, use the Edit button on that beat below. Nothing goes live until you press Publish changes.

Fill in genre and BPM from a list

Paste a list of beats with their BPM (for example copied from BeatStars)

Paste the whole list as it is. Each beat name is matched to a beat in your store, and the BPM that follows it is picked up. Nothing is changed until you press Apply, and nothing goes live until you press Publish changes.

Your beats

Fill in only what you want to change. Empty boxes are left as they are.
Give the selected beats their own price. Empty boxes are left as they are.

Drag the ⋮⋮ handle to move a beat (drag a selected beat to move all selected beats together). On a keyboard, focus the handle and press the up or down arrow. Tick the boxes (or hold Ctrl or Shift and click a row) to change several beats at once. "Remove" takes a beat off the site, and deletes its preview file when you publish.
